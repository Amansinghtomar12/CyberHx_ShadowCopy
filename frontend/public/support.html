Support

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn’t, email us — during an event we watch the inbox.

01Quick answers

I was sent an invite link. What do I do?
Open it. If you already have an account, sign in and you’ll be asked to join the team. If you don’t, register — you’ll be placed on the team automatically. No code to type.
The board says “Join or create a team first”.
This event runs in team mode. Open Team in the header, then create a team (a team of one is fine) or join one with a link or code from your captain.
“Too fast. Wait 9s.”
There is a ten-second cooldown per challenge between submissions. It stops brute force; think, then submit.
“Terminal locked: maximum attempts reached”.
Each challenge has an attempt limit set by the organisers. Once you hit it, that challenge is closed to you. Rejected submissions during the cooldown do not count against it.
Submissions are closed / the event hasn’t started.
The clock in the board header is the truth. Before the start you can read every brief; the flag form appears the moment the countdown ends.
I forgot my password.
There is no self-service reset yet. Email us from the address you registered with and we’ll sort it. If you registered with Google, just use “Continue with Google”.
“Human verification unavailable” on the sign-in page.
Your browser could not reach challenges.cloudflare.com. An ad blocker, privacy extension, VPN or a locked-down network is the usual cause. Allow it for this site, or switch network, then press “Try again”.
Can I turn the sound off?
Yes — the speaker icon in the header, or Settings → Interface sound. It’s remembered on that device.
Something on the scoreboard looks wrong.
It refreshes every 15 seconds, and may be frozen near the end of an event (it says so at the top). If a solve is missing after a minute, email us with the team, challenge and time.
