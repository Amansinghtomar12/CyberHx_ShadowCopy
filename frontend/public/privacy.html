PRIVACY POLICY

CyberHX CTF Platform · last updated 25 August 2026

CyberHX CTF is a Capture The Flag competition platform. This page explains what personal data the platform collects, why it is collected, and how to have it removed.

What we collect

When you create an account, we store:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and for sign-in.
  • Username — shown publicly on the scoreboard and team pages.
  • Password — stored only as a salted hash; never in readable form.
  • Optional profile details you choose to add: country, affiliation, website, biography, avatar.

While you play, we also record:

  • Flag submissions — including incorrect attempts, to enforce attempt limits and detect abuse.
  • Solves, points and timestamps — required to rank the scoreboard.
  • Team membership and hints you unlock.

If you sign in with Google

Choosing "Continue with Google" shares only your email address and basic profile (name and picture) with us. We never receive your Google password, and we request no access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or any other Google service.

What is public

Your username, avatar, country, affiliation, team, score and solve times appear on the public scoreboard. Your email address is never shown publicly to other players. You may hide your profile from public listings from your account settings.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — database, authentication and server functions.
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery.
  • Cloudflare — bot protection (Turnstile) on the sign-in form.

These providers process data on our behalf so the platform can run. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Cookies

We use only what is required to keep you signed in — a session token stored by your browser. There are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Account and competition data is retained while your account exists, so that scoreboards and results stay accurate. Delete your account and we remove your personal data; anonymised competition statistics may remain.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correction of anything inaccurate, or deletion of your account. Contact us and we will action it.
